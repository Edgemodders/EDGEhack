# Changelog

What changed in each EDGEhack release, for players. Each release takes its notes from its section here: the GitHub release, the Marketplace's changelog tab and the version's changelog on the Nexus Mods page.

How to keep it (the Edgemodders convention):

- With each change a player would notice, add a line under **Unreleased**, in its group: **New**, **Changed**, **Fixed** or **Other**. One change per line. Leave out test-only changes.
- Before a release, rename **Unreleased** to `## <version> (<date>)` and start a new empty **Unreleased** above it.
- Links are full URLs, since the same text goes to GitHub and Nexus.

## Unreleased

**New**

- **Icons of your own:** **Upload** on a quickhack's Item, Wheel and Status icons takes a PNG or JPG and fits it to the game's size for that icon (99 x 128, 64 x 64 and 64 x 64). For a white picture on black, **Black is see-through** turns the black into transparency, as the game's wheel and status icons are. Saving puts the pictures into one texture with the game's UI setup and an atlas in your project, and points the quickhack at them; **Original** goes back to the game's icon. A new quickhack's status icon goes on its own copy of the status effect, so the game's quickhack keeps its icon. Needs REDcode 0.10.8 or later.
- **Generate** on each icon: OpenAI's GPT Image draws it from your words ("a skull made of static") in that icon's look, and the result shows at its real size beside the icon shown now, with **Keep** and **Try Again**; nothing goes into the mod until Keep. **Generate All** draws the Item, then the Wheel and Status from it, so all three show the same glyph. **Show the Prompt** shows the whole prompt sent. It uses your own OpenAI key and credits (each Generate is one paid image), kept in the EDGE tools' shared key store and asked for once; **EDGEhack: Set the OpenAI Key** changes it. Settings choose the model and quality.
- **The Desk:** quickhacks you work on outside a project, kept between sessions. **Clone and Modify on the Desk** (the Desk view's **+**) starts one from any of the game's quickhacks, and with no project open in REDcode, Clone and Modify and Edit land there too. A quickhack on the Desk writes no mod files; **Add to Project** puts it into your open REDcode project under the project's names and writes its files, and adding it again replaces the project's copy after asking. **Duplicate** copies a new quickhack under another name, and **Remove from the Desk** moves it to the Recycle Bin. The Desk view also lists the open project's quickhacks. Hovering a Desk entry shows **Add to Project** (the new-file icon, as on EDGEfab's Desk) and a trash can that removes it, as the right-click menu does. A quickhack in the Desk view's Project group has a trash can too: **Remove from Project** moves its document and mod files to the Recycle Bin and takes its texts out of the project, after asking; a copy on the Desk stays.
- **Get it** on a new quickhack's page: each tier is sold the way the game's tier it started from is, by the same vendors behind the same gate (V's level, or the cyberdeck's quality), as the quickhack itself or as a schematic of its own that teaches V to craft it. The page says how the game's tier is got. **Sell it there** takes a tier out of the shops, and **Components to craft it** changes how many components crafting it takes. Vendors show it on a save you already have.
- **Add Effect** in the quickhack editor: **Copy Existing** adds another quickhack's effect, which follows that quickhack's tiers, and **Status Effects** any status effect, sorted by who it works on and searchable by name, tag and type. Each added effect can be left off some tiers, and has its own duration, stacks and damage per tier; a changed number gives that tier a copy of its own. The editor warns about combinations that may not work, and notes what the game does with others, as it should (a burn ends Contagion's poison when V has Contagion Epic or better).
- **Make New** in Add Effect: a damage over time (Electric, Thermal, Chemical or Physical, with damage a hit, seconds between hits and how long; electric can show the game's sparks as it starts), a stat change while hacked (accuracy, armor, a resistance, evasion, detection, time between shots, or no sprinting, jumping, cover or calling for help), or a chance to apply any status effect, each with its numbers per tier. Chemical and physical damage stay off machines, as an added poison or bleed does.
- When EDGEhack adds a file to your project (a quickhack's YAML, its texts file, the shared script), a message names it, with **Open** to see it as text.
- A poison or bleed added to a quickhack keeps its damage off machines (androids, drones and mechs), which the game would let it hit through a quickhack that can target them. The editor says so in a note.
- **Clone and Modify** on a quickhack's page and in the Quickhacks view: a new quickhack beside the game's, with its own name, item per tier, wheel entry and cooldown, which leaves the game's quickhack as it is. Spread and the versions for bosses are left out for now.
- The Quickhacks view lists the open project's quickhacks in a Project group at the top.
- The EDGEhack side bar with its Desk, Library and Quickhacks views (Desk and Library empty until their phases).
- The Quickhacks view: the game's quickhacks by category, family and tier, with their icons, then device and vehicle hacks; a filter.
- The quickhack page (read-only): a quickhack's texts, icons, RAM, upload, cooldown, duration and damage for every tier, its effects in plain words, who it works on, spread, what it does while equipped, its crafting spec, and every record behind it, each opening in REDcode.
- Edit a game quickhack: its name, description, wheel text, item and wheel icons (from the game's own), RAM, upload, duration, damage and cooldown, per tier where the game allows, with All tiers and the game's value beside each. Each save writes a TweakXL and ArchiveXL mod into your REDcode project; Pack and Play is on the page.
- Open in EDGEhack on any record in REDcode's TweakDB view, and the command EDGEhack: Open a Quickhack..., which take any record of a quickhack (any tier, its Boss, Chimera or Spread action, its crafting spec).
