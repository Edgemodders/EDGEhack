# EDGEhack

A quickhack editor for Cyberpunk 2077, in the EDGE family of modding tools. Open any of the game's quickhacks and see every tier in plain words. Change its costs, texts and effects, or make new quickhacks with effects, icons and logic of your own, and try them live in the running game.

> **0.1.0, the first release, for game 2.5.** A preview: every part passed its tests, in the game where it changes the game. [Guide](https://edgemodders.ai/wiki/edgehack/)

## What it does

- **Open any quickhack** from the Quickhacks view, or by clicking one in REDcode, and see every tier's name, icon, RAM, upload time, cooldown, duration, damage and effects in plain words.
- **Change it:** texts, icons, costs and numbers, for one tier or every tier at once. Your numbers are kept as numbers, so they survive game patches.
- **Make new ones:** **Clone and Modify** a game quickhack into a new one beside it, sold and crafted the way its source tier is.
- **Add effects:** another quickhack's effect, any status effect on the target or on V, or one EDGEhack builds: a damage over time, a stat change, a chance to apply.
- **Icons of your own:** upload pictures, or have OpenAI's GPT Image draw them from your words (your own OpenAI key).
- **Scripts:** a scan check and an on-land function in JavaScript, which REDnode runs in the game.
- **Feature lines:** bullets of your own in the item's tooltip.
- **Try Live:** a change in the running game through REDnode, with no packing; **Stop** puts the game back.
- **The Desk and the EDGE Library:** work outside a project, and keep quickhacks for good to take into any mod, with **Refresh from Library**.
- **Assistants:** **Connect an Assistant (MCP)...** gives Claude Code EDGEhack's tools; REDagent gets them in the game through REDnode. They ask you before every change.
- **A mod you can read:** TweakXL and ArchiveXL files in your REDcode project.

## What it needs

- Windows, Cyberpunk 2077 game 2.5, and REDcode 0.10.8 or later (it installs with EDGEhack).
- The mods it makes need TweakXL, ArchiveXL and redscript. Scripts need REDnode 0.61.1 or later; Try Live needs 0.62.0.
