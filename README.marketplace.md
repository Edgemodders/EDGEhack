# EDGEhack

A quickhack editor for Cyberpunk 2077, in the EDGE family of modding tools.

> **Preview, not ready for use yet.** This first build has the side bar and its views only. The plan is in the [design](https://edgemodders.github.io/EDGEhack/design.html).

- **Open any quickhack** from the Quickhacks view, or by clicking one in REDcode, and see its name, icon, tier, RAM, upload time, cooldown, duration, damage and effects in plain words.
- **Change it:** texts, icons, costs and numbers, for one tier or the whole family.
- **Make new ones** from a game quickhack, adding buffs, debuffs and attack features from other quickhacks or the game's status effects.
- **A mod you can read:** TweakXL and ArchiveXL files in your REDcode project.

Needs Windows, Cyberpunk 2077, and REDcode. The mods it makes need TweakXL and ArchiveXL.
